A "page" means a page or an equivalent pre-defined section (for example a gallery or testimonials block). Prices are the setup amount payable; any applicable tax is shown on your quote and invoice.

Included in every package, for the first year

  • Twelve months of managed static hosting with valid HTTPS (subject to acceptable-use terms)
  • One available, standard-price .co.ke domain registered in your name for the first year (availability and registry conditions apply)
  • Mobile-responsive design, WhatsApp/phone contact and basic SEO
  • A private preview to approve before anything goes public
  • Handover details when your site is live

Delivery target: within 7 days after payment is verified and your minimum content is complete. We pause the clock while we wait for you (content, preview feedback, final payment) or for the domain registry.

Renewal, in plain words

From year two, renewal covers: standard domain renewal (agreed tld, registrar pricing applies); managed static hosting and https; basic infrastructure support.

Not included in renewal: unlimited text or photo edits; new pages or a redesign; online checkout or ecommerce; 24/7 bespoke support. These can be added as itemised extras that you approve first.

Renewal prices are proposed and may change if registrar or hosting costs change. We give at least 60 days’ written notice and you can decline and take your domain elsewhere.

How payment works

The default is a 50% deposit to start and the balance after you approve your preview, before your site goes public. We only start once your deposit is verified, and a payment screenshot alone is not treated as payment. Payment instructions are confirmed with you when you place your order.
