How custom projects work

A process designed to avoid surprises

  1. Discovery

    A conversation (and often a workshop) to understand goals, users, data and constraints.

  2. Proposal

    A written, itemised quotation: scope, assumptions, exclusions, schedule, risks and total.

  3. Contract

    Milestones, payment schedule and acceptance criteria agreed before work begins.

  4. Delivery

    Design, build, quality and security review, and user acceptance with you at each stage.

  5. Support

    Launch, handover, warranty period, then maintenance and improvements on agreed terms.

What a quotation contains

  • Scope and acceptance criteria
  • Itemised effort, licences, third-party services and hosting
  • Milestone amounts and schedule
  • Assumptions, exclusions and risks
  • Tax treatment, validity period and support terms

Changes along the way

Every project changes. When it does, we write the change down, price it, and get your approval before doing it. No silent scope creep, in either direction.

No price ceiling

Projects can be any size, from tens of thousands of shillings to many millions. Budget is optional on the quote form, and "not sure" is a perfectly good answer.
