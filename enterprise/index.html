Working with larger organisations

Built for procurement, governance and accountability

Large projects need paperwork and traceability as much as code. Here is what we plan to provide on engagements; each is agreed in the contract.

Commercial clarity

Itemised quotations, milestone invoicing, retainers for support, and written change control.

Named stakeholders

Project roles for your sponsor, approvers and users, with separate access for each.

Traceable work

Approvals, deliverables and changes are recorded, so decisions can be audited later.

Security by design

Role-based access, least-privilege credentials and a security review before launch. We do not claim formal certifications we do not hold.

Data protection

We work with you to meet your obligations under Kenyan data-protection law, with legal review where needed.

Long-term support

Warranty after launch, then maintenance and improvement on terms you choose.

Typical engagement

From first conversation to production

  1. Discovery and scoping

    Workshops with your stakeholders, requirements, risks and constraints.

  2. Proposal and contract

    Detailed quotation, milestones, acceptance criteria, security and support terms.

  3. Build in stages

    Design approval, development, quality and security review, user acceptance.

  4. Launch and handover

    Production release, documentation, training and warranty period.

  5. Support and growth

    Managed hosting, maintenance and new phases as your needs grow.
