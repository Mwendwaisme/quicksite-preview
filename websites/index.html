Delivery target: within 7 days after payment is verified and your minimum content is complete. We pause the clock while we wait for you (content, preview feedback, final payment) or for the domain registry.

Renewal, in plain words

From year two, renewal covers: standard domain renewal (agreed tld, registrar pricing applies); managed static hosting and https; basic infrastructure support.

Not included in renewal: unlimited text or photo edits; new pages or a redesign; online checkout or ecommerce; 24/7 bespoke support. These can be added as itemised extras that you approve first.

Renewal prices are proposed and may change if registrar or hosting costs change. We give at least 60 days’ written notice and you can decline and take your domain elsewhere.

Good to know

What Express is, and what it is not

Express is for

  • Shops, stalls, salons, restaurants and services
  • Schools, churches and small organisations
  • Freelancers and creators
  • Anyone who needs to be found and contacted on WhatsApp

Not part of Express

These need a custom quotation. You can start with Express and grow into them.

  • Online checkout and self-service ecommerce
  • Databases, logins and server-side back ends
  • Stock synchronisation with POS or inventory systems
  • ERP, MIS, LMS or other business systems
  • Custom APIs and integrations
  • Mobile apps and bespoke workflow engines
  • Unlimited revisions
